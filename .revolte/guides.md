# DealerFlow conventions

Premium, quiet dealership workspace. Light B2B preset customized to the supplied PRD: Inter, warm off-white, slate, cobalt; compact 8px rhythm and 12–16px surfaces. Tailwind semantic tokens only; Lucide icons; original representative vehicle SVGs. React/Vite SPA with Express API. PostgreSQL only, integer paise, UTC timestamps and Asia/Kolkata business days. RBAC is independent of branch filters and presentation presets.
